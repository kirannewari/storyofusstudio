    ABOUT US

    Story of Us Studio is a wedding photography and cinematography collective rooted in Loharu, built on the belief that weddings are not just events, but stories meant to be felt and remembered.
    Founded and led by Mr. Hemant & Mrs. Sweety, this husband–wife duo brings a rare blend of emotion and professionalism to every wedding they capture. Supported by Mr. Pawan (our Butterfly), the creative force behind the scenes, the team ensures that every moment — big or small — is documented with care and perfection. From intimate hometown weddings to grand destination celebrations, we have captured 50+ wedding stories across Haryana, Delhi, and Rajasthan, covering cities like Jaipur, Udaipur, Kota, Ajmer, Loharu, Hisar, Bhiwani, Panipat, Rewari, Gurgaon, Alwar, and many more. We would love to be a part of your journey and create visuals that don’t just look beautiful — but feel like you.
